## What an invitation code does — and what it doesn't

Two separate things get tangled together whenever people talk about invite codes, and separating them makes the whole picture much clearer.

**What the code determines:** the fee rebate you receive on your own trading, and it seals the referral relationship. Whoever shared the code earns a commission on your trading fees, and the referrer decides how much of that commission to pass back to you. That pass-back is what shows up as your rebate. The code also determines which referral program you're enrolled in — Gate runs a Lite mode and a Pro mode, and you can only be in one of them. **What the code does not do:** it doesn't move you up the VIP ladder, and it doesn't change your base maker/taker rates. Those are set by your 30-day volume, your 14-day average GT holdings, or your account asset value — all things you control after signup, not before. A code promising you VIP 5 is promising something the system doesn't work that way.

If you want to see the exact rebate percentage attached to your account rather than taking anyone's word for it, it's visible on your own referral page once you're registered. That's the number that matters, and it's the only one that can't be fudged.

## What quietly voids the code or the rewards

- **Registering first and entering the code after.** Nothing can be done about it. Support can't backfill a referral relationship.
- **Deleting an account to re-register with a better code.** Gate's rules treat this as abuse, and the new account loses all commission and referral rights. Duplicate accounts sharing the same KYC identity get flagged and their commission is terminated.
- **Depositing through a channel the campaign excludes.** On-chain transfers, fiat deposits and P2P purchases typically count. Internal transfers, GateCode transfers and gift coins don't, and card or third-party on-ramp payments are treated inconsistently between campaigns — the rule text on the live page wins.
- **Trading stablecoin pairs to farm volume.** Stablecoin pairs are excluded from the invite-side trading volume statistics, so the USDC/USDT pair generates no credit whatsoever.
- **Forgetting to claim.** Vouchers sit in the coupon center waiting for you, with short validity windows attached. Rewards that disappear unclaimed are, functionally, rewards you declined.
- **Withdrawing too eagerly.** Campaign terms include holding periods — often a couple of hours on the qualifying deposit — and accounts that pull funds during the window can fail the task.
